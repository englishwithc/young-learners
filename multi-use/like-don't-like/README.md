# 🍽️ The Yummy Yum Cafe — "I like / I don't like"

A super-fun interactive classroom game for practising **"I like…"** and **"I don't like…"** with young English learners. Fully working offline — no installation, no accounts, no internet needed after the first load (which isn't even needed if fonts are cached!).

## 🎮 How the game works

1. **Pick a world** — Food & Drink (cafe), Animals (zoo), Colours (art studio), or Other (magic stage — any words!).
2. **Set up** — add 2–6 players (name + animal avatar + colour), then choose items:
   - ✅ **Built-in pack** — 12 ready-made emoji items per theme. Play instantly!
   - 🖼️ **Your own pictures** — drop AI-generated images (or any images) into the upload zone. Names are optional — mystery items are fun too! Your uploads are remembered for next time.
3. **Vote!** — each food/animal/colour appears with a big reveal. Students vote:
   - 👍 / 👎 buttons on each player card, **or**
   - 🖐️ drag the item onto a player who likes it (or onto the bin!)
   - The livestats at the top count the class votes.
4. **The verdict** — more likes → confetti, applause, and the item slides into the cafe display/menu tray! More dislikes → trash-splat full-screen silliness with sad trombone. A tie → teacher's big 🟢 KEEP IT / 🔴 TRASH IT decision!
5. **Grand finale** — the finished display case, imaginary customers reacting, the **Hall of Fame** (every player's "I like…" collection), silly awards, and a recap of every "I like / I don't like" sentence said.


## 🖼️ Getting funny food pictures

Use your favourite images online or use an AI image generator with prompts like:

- "a cartoon banana riding a tiny skateboard, kawaii style, white background"
- "spaghetti doughnut with sprinkles, cartoon clipart, white background"
- "a milk carton with legs doing a silly dance, flat vector, white background"

**Tip:** White (or plain-colour) backgrounds look cleanest when they pop onto the pedestal!
