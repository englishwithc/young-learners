Preposition Dash

A force-field bubble floats in the arena. There are four places you can be: on the bubble, in it, under it, or by it. Each place is a lane drawn on the floor. When a lane flashes red, something is about to travel through it.

CRUSHER Drops straight down, breaks the crown, jams inside. Hits ON + IN.
DART A spear that skewers the shell through the middle and punches out the far side. Hits IN + BY.
FLOOD A tide that climbs over the floor of the bubble and past the right column. Hits UNDER + BY.
SPLIT COMETS One rides the top lane, one rides the bottom lane. Hits ON + UNDER.
WRECKING BALL Swings over the crown and down the right column. Hits ON + BY.
ERUPTOR Spikes climb out of the ground and stab up through the floor of the shell to the middle. Hits IN + UNDER.

Two lanes are always red and two are always clear, so no single word works every time. The red lane is the only place a hit can land.

Chill gives you about five seconds per wave.

Click a command or press 1–4. Press P or Escape to pause. Press A to hide the red lane markers and play from memory.
