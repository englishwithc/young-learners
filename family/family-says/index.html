🕵️ Imposter Mode A sneaky Simon-Says-style twist. ⌄

Imposter Mode

The imposter stays until you tap 🔁. When they turn evil, freeze and don't do the action.

How it works: The first imposter appears after 3 actions so everyone can see the trick. After that, that same family member stays the imposter until you tap 🔁 New imposter in the top bar.

Tapping 🔁 removes the current imposter straight away, and a new one is revealed 3 actions later. When the imposter gives a command, their face turns evil halfway through — 🚫 freeze and don't do the action.

The imposter also shows up a little more often than everyone else, so watch out!
